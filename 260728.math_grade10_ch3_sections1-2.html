18（6分）用单调性定义证明：函数 \\(f(x)=x+\\dfrac{1}{x}\\) 在区间 \\((0,\\;1)\\) 上单调递减。
【参考证明】

证明：设 \\(0 < x_{1} < x_{2} < 1\\)，

\\(f(x_{1})-f(x_{2})=\\left(x_{1}+\\dfrac{1}{x_{1}}\\right)-\\left(x_{2}+\\dfrac{1}{x_{2}}\\right)\\)

\\(=(x_{1}-x_{2})+\\left(\\dfrac{1}{x_{1}}-\\dfrac{1}{x_{2}}\\right)=(x_{1}-x_{2})+\\dfrac{x_{2}-x_{1}}{x_{1}x_{2}}=(x_{1}-x_{2})\\left(1-\\dfrac{1}{x_{1}x_{2}}\\right)\\)

\\(=\\dfrac{(x_{1}-x_{2})(x_{1}x_{2}-1)}{x_{1}x_{2}}\\)

∵ \\(0 < x_{1} < x_{2} < 1\\)，∴ \\(x_{1}-x_{2} < 0\\)，\\(x_{1}x_{2}-1 < 0\\)，\\(x_{1}x_{2} > 0\\)

∴ \\(f(x_{1})-f(x_{2}) > 0\\)，即 \\(f(x_{1}) > f(x_{2})\\)

故 \\(f(x)=x+\\dfrac{1}{x}\\) 在 \\((0,\\;1)\\) 上单调递减。
