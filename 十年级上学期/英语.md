## Unit 4 — Natural Disasters

### Topics
- Types of natural disasters
- Emergency preparedness & rescue

### Key Vocabulary
- `disaster`, `tornado`, `drought`, `landslide`, `tsunami`
- `evacuate`, `shelter`, `ruin`, `destroy`, `survive`, `survivor`
- `supply`, `effort`, `emergency`, `calm down`
- `in shock`, `in ruins`, `in the open air`

### Key Grammar
- **限制性定语从句（关系代词）**
  - *who / whom / which / that / whose*
  - 指人：who/that/whom；指物：which/that
  - 关系代词作主语/宾语/定语

### Reading
- *The Night the Earth Didn't Sleep* — 唐山大地震报道
- 时间顺序（chronological order）组织方式

### Writing
- 写新闻报道（News Report）
  - 标题 + 导语 + 正文（5W1H）

## Unit 5 — Languages Around the World

### Topics
- World languages, Chinese writing system
- Language learning strategies

### Key Vocabulary
- `native`, `attitude`, `reference`, `refer to`, `system`
- `struggle`, `dialect`, `calligraphy`, `global`, `affair`
- `despite`, `factor`, `base`, `date back to`, `ups and downs`
- `major`, `means`, `regard...as`, `be known for`

### Key Grammar
- **限制性定语从句（关系副词）**
  - *when / where / why*
  - *when* = 表时间 = `in/on/at which`
  - *where* = 表地点 = `in/at which`
  - *why* = 表原因 = `for which`
- 关系副词与关系代词的区分

### Reading
- *The Chinese Writing System: Connecting the Past and the Present*
- 说明文阅读策略

### Writing
- 写博客/观点短文（语言学习相关）
- 表达观点与依据的衔接词

## 语法体系汇总

| 项目 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 词法 | 名词短语、形容词短语、副词短语 |
| 时态 | 一般现在/过去/将来时、现在进行时（表将来） |
| 定语从句 | 关系代词（who/whom/which/that/whose）|
| 定语从句 | 关系副词（when/where/why）|
| 比较结构 | 比较级、最高级、同级比较 |
| 重点句型 | It is + adj. + to do, so...that..., 强调句 |
